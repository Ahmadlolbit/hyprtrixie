# Everyday things

## Screenshots

| Keys | Takes |
| --- | --- |
| Print | a region you drag |
| Shift + Print | the window |
| Ctrl + Print | the whole screen |

They go to `~/Pictures/Screenshots` and the clipboard. Click **Edit** on the notification to draw on one.

## Lock and idle

- Super + L locks.
- After 3 minutes the screensaver starts, at 5 the screen locks, at 30 the laptop suspends. The setup menu changes the minutes.
- Menu › Toggle › Stay awake stops that until you turn it off; the bar shows it.
- Menu › Toggle › Screensaver turns the screensaver off or on.

## Notifications

Menu › Toggle › Do not disturb hides them until you turn it off.

## The bar

Click a widget for more: the clock for a calendar, the network for Wi-Fi, the speaker for audio devices, the battery for its health and the power mode. Scroll on the speaker or the brightness to change them.
